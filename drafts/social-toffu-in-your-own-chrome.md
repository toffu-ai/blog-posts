## LinkedIn, Toffu company page (English)

```
Every agency has data behind a login with no API: the client's reporting portal, a Looker Studio report someone else built, a partner dashboard.

Until now, that part ended with "export a CSV and upload it."

Today we release the Toffu Chrome extension. It lets Toffu control your own Chrome, with the logins and sessions you already have.

Install it and ask:
"Use my own Chrome. Open the client portal and compare October's leads with what Google Ads reports."

→ Toffu works in one tab only: the purple Toffu tab group. Your other tabs stay out of reach.
→ You see every click. Toffu's cursor moves to each button before it clicks.
→ Cancel on Chrome's bar stops it at once.
→ It never types a password. Logins and 2FA codes come back to you in the chat.

Not the same as Toffu's Cloud Browser, which runs on our servers and keeps working when your computer is off. The extension is for the sites that need your own session.

Part of the Browser add-on. How it works: https://toffu.ai/blog/toffu-in-your-own-chrome
```
