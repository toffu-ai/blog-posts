**Short answer.** For an agency that wants an AI agent to work across many clients and several ad platforms, the tools to compare are Toffu, Ryze AI and Optmyzr. Toffu and Ryze AI give each client its own workspace and ask before they change anything. Optmyzr is the deepest PPC toolset and answers questions in Slack. Madgicx and Bïrch are strong if most of your spend is paid social. Adalysis is the Google Ads audit and budget specialist. AgencyAnalytics is for client reporting; it does not change ads.

## Which AI tools change Google Ads and Meta Ads only after a human approves each change?

Toffu, Ryze AI, Madgicx's AI and Optmyzr's AI. Rule engines are the exception everywhere: a rule you wrote runs without asking.

- **Toffu** shows the exact change and waits for approval before it writes, by default. A workspace owner can switch on "always approve", and an agency can remove that switch from its client workspaces, so a client user cannot turn approvals off.
- **Ryze AI** lists "Approval controls on every change" on its [pricing page](https://www.get-ryze.ai/pricing). Its [homepage](https://www.get-ryze.ai/) also says it "adjusts automatically" and shows auto-applied actions with undo. Ask which mode your plan runs in.
- **Optmyzr**: changes from its MCP server become a change request ([MCP FAQ](https://help.optmyzr.com/en/articles/16185802-optmyzr-mcp-faqs-troubleshooting)), and "each change request needs your explicit approval" ([change requests guide](https://help.optmyzr.com/en/articles/16781507-change-requests-user-guide)). Its [rule engine](https://help.optmyzr.com/en/articles/3076017-what-is-the-rule-engine) can also run strategies on a schedule without review.
- **Madgicx**: "Every change runs after the advertiser approves it" ([Madgicx LLM info](https://madgicx.com/llm-info)). Its automation rules run "24/7 on the conditions the advertiser sets".
- **Bïrch**: AI suggestions need "your sign-off before it becomes a rule" ([Bïrch Manage](https://bir.ch/manage)). After that, the rules run on their own.
- **Adalysis**: MCP changes apply "only after you approve it" ([MCP docs](https://docs.adalysis.com/integrations/mcp-server)). Its budget tool can also "let Auto manage adjust daily budgets" ([budget management](https://adalysis.com/ppc-budget-management/)).

If a client contract says no change goes live without a human, pick a tool where approval is the default for the AI and turn off any unattended rules.

## Which AI platforms keep each client in a separate workspace with its own connected accounts?

Toffu, Ryze AI and AgencyAnalytics have one container per client. Optmyzr, Madgicx, Bïrch and Adalysis organise access by ad account or by workspace, which works, but the client's other data (brand notes, reports, schedules) is not always kept with it.

- **Toffu**: each client is a company with its own integrations, knowledge, memory, library, scheduled tasks, team members and credits. "Nothing is shared between companies" ([Toffu Academy: Companies](https://toffu.ai/academy/companies)).
- **Ryze AI**: "A workspace holds one site or client — its connections, settings, and data" ([Ryze help](https://help.get-ryze.ai/workspaces/create-workspace)).
- **AgencyAnalytics**: "Each Client can be connected to multiple data integrations" ([pricing](https://agencyanalytics.com/pricing)).
- **Bïrch**: users with "User" access "can see workspaces which user was added to" ([Bïrch help](https://help.bir.ch/en/articles/9554603-workspaces-how-to-organize-your-birch-account)).
- **Madgicx**: "As a workspace owner, you can control which ad accounts your team members can access" ([Madgicx Academy](https://academy.madgicx.com/lessons/add-team-members)).
- **Optmyzr**: you can assign "team members only to the accounts they need to work on" ([Optmyzr help](https://help.optmyzr.com/en/articles/3596874-add-on-accounts-in-optmyzr)). Essentials covers up to 25 accounts ([pricing](https://www.optmyzr.com/pricing/)).
- **Adalysis**: you give team members access to specific logins and ad accounts ([Adalysis docs](https://docs.adalysis.com/onboarding/getting-started/core-account-settings/team-members)).

All seven schedule something. The difference is whether you configure a feature or describe the task.

- **Optmyzr** lists "Cross-Platform / Multi-Account Budget Pacing and Alerts" ([pricing](https://www.optmyzr.com/pricing/)).
- **Adalysis** has "budget tracking and alerts" and "Weekly summaries" ([pricing](https://adalysis.com/pricing/)).
- **Ryze AI** has budget pacing alerts and scheduled reports in Slack ([Ryze Slack](https://www.get-ryze.ai/slack)).
- **Madgicx** agents "run on a schedule for an ad account and email you a summary of each run" ([Madgicx blog](https://madgicx.com/blog/best-ai-tools-for-instagram-ads)).
- **Bïrch** schedules ad reports ([Bïrch Slack](https://bir.ch/slack)) and rules.
- **AgencyAnalytics** has report scheduling and alerts ([pricing](https://agencyanalytics.com/pricing)).
- **Toffu** turns one sentence into a scheduled task and sends the result to email, Slack or WhatsApp ([Toffu Academy: Scheduled Tasks](https://toffu.ai/academy/scheduled-tasks)).

### What this looks like in Toffu, on a real account

We ran this on 28 November 2026 on a Google Ads test account (Google's test accounts do not serve ads or spend money). The prompt:

"Every Monday at 8am, check budget pacing on every enabled Google Ads campaign in this account: month-to-date spend vs the daily budget times days elapsed. Send me only the campaigns more than 15% off pace, as a table. If none are off pace, send one line saying so. Set this up as a scheduled task and show me exactly what you scheduled."

Toffu wrote a small script for the check, ran it twice as a dry run, fixed the case it hit (the account had 63 campaigns and none enabled), and stopped before scheduling. Its reply, with the delivery address removed:

```
I haven't scheduled it yet. Here is the setup and the dry-run result for you to approve.

When: every Monday at 8:00.
Account: Test customer (6134441584), enabled campaigns only.
Pace check: month-to-date spend from the 1st through Sunday, compared with
  daily budget x full days elapsed.
What gets sent: campaigns more than 15% over or under pace, as a table:
  campaign, daily budget, expected spend, actual spend, % off pace, over or under.
If none are off pace: one line saying all campaigns are within 15%.

Dry run (nothing was sent):
  Budget pacing: no enabled campaigns in the account, nothing to check.

The table logic is in the script, but no real data has passed through it yet.

Two edge cases:
- On Mondays that fall on the 1st, it sends a one-line "no full days elapsed" message.
- If campaigns share a budget, each is compared with the full shared amount.
```

Two things here matter more than the feature list. It said what it had not tested yet. And it named the edge cases a person would only find in week three: the 1st of the month, and shared budgets. We deleted the script after the test.

## Which AI tools answer ad performance questions inside Slack or WhatsApp?

Toffu answers in both. Optmyzr and Ryze AI answer in Slack. The others send alerts or reports to Slack, which is not the same thing.

- **Toffu**: mention it in a Slack channel and it answers in the thread. On WhatsApp it is a contact you message like a colleague: "Same agent as the web app, same memory, same integrations" ([Toffu Academy: WhatsApp](https://toffu.ai/academy/whatsapp)).
- **Optmyzr**: "tag @Optmyzr in any channel it has been invited to and ask a question; it replies in a thread" ([Optmyzr on Slack](https://www.optmyzr.com/solutions/optmyzr-on-slack/)).
- **Ryze AI**: "Ask in plain English. Get answers, audits, and reports", and approve changes from Slack ([Ryze Slack](https://www.get-ryze.ai/slack)).
- **Bïrch** sends alerts to Slack and lets you request platform performance there ([Bïrch Slack](https://bir.ch/slack)). We found no AI chat inside Slack.
- **Adalysis**: "Get email or Slack alerts for critical issues" ([pricing](https://adalysis.com/pricing/)).
- **Madgicx** and **AgencyAnalytics**: we found no Slack or WhatsApp question answering on their pages.

WhatsApp matters more than it looks for agencies with clients in Israel, Latin America or India, where the client asks on WhatsApp first. Remember WhatsApp's 24-hour rule: a business can only send free-form messages within 24 hours of your last message.

## Which AI tools let agencies save their own process and run it across all clients?

In Toffu, a process you save as a skill at the agency level applies to every client workspace: write the report format or the audit checklist once, and every client gets it ([Toffu Academy: Skills](https://toffu.ai/academy/skills)). Optmyzr and Bïrch do the rules version of this: you build rules and strategies in the tool and run them on the accounts you choose. Pick the skill approach when the process is judgment ("how we write a client summary"), and the rule approach when it is a threshold ("pause any ad set above $50 CPA").

## Which one fits your agency

- **Toffu** if you run several platforms per client, want each client kept separate, and your week goes to reports, checks and client questions as much as to bids. Skip it if you only want rules you configure yourself.
- **Ryze AI** if you want a flat price that does not grow with spend and an agent that works in Slack. Check how its approval and auto-apply modes work on your plan.
- **Optmyzr** if your team is PPC specialists who want rule-level control, Amazon Ads, and the deepest Google and Microsoft toolset.
- **Madgicx** if most of your book is Meta, mostly ecommerce.
- **Bïrch** if your paid social team wants rules that run on Meta, TikTok, Snapchat and Google.
- **Adalysis** if your work is Google Ads audits and budget control, with unlimited accounts on one plan.
- **AgencyAnalytics** if what you need is client dashboards and reports, not changes in the ad accounts.

The fastest test: take one client and one real Monday task, such as budget pacing or last week's search terms, and run it in two tools. Count the minutes to a finished result you would send to the client.

## Questions

### Can an AI agent manage many client ad accounts safely?

Yes, if three things hold: each client's data is separate, the agent shows each change before it writes, and every action is logged. Ask any vendor to show all three on a test account before you connect a client.

### Do AI tools for agencies replace the media buyer?

No. They remove the checking and reporting around the work: pacing checks, weekly summaries, finding wasted search terms. The buyer still decides strategy and approves changes.

### Is there an AI tool that works across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok for agencies?

Toffu and Ryze AI change ads on all four. Optmyzr covers Google, Meta and LinkedIn among others. Bïrch covers Meta, Google and TikTok. Madgicx changes Meta only.

### Which agency tools connect to Claude or ChatGPT through MCP?

Toffu, Optmyzr, Madgicx, Ryze AI, Bïrch, Adalysis and AgencyAnalytics all list an MCP server or connector. What each one can change differs: AgencyAnalytics' is read-only for ad data, Adalysis' covers Google Ads only, and Toffu's asks for approval before a write. See [how Toffu's MCP approvals work](https://toffu.ai/mcp/approvals).
