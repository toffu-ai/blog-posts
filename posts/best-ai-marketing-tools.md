## The difference that matters: assistants vs agents

Almost every tool above is an **assistant**. It makes one step faster, and you still carry the work from tool to tool: export from Ads, paste into a sheet, write the summary, send it.

An **AI marketing agent** takes the goal and does the steps itself, inside your connected accounts: pull the data, compare periods, find the problem, propose the fix, and apply it when you approve.

If your team uses many AI tools and still spends hours a week on reporting and campaign checks, you have plenty of assistants and no agent.

## The picks, one by one

### Toffu: run the work, not one step
**Best for:** agencies and in-house teams that run Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and reporting across several accounts.
Toffu connects to your ad platforms, analytics, Search Console and SEO tools, and works in them from one chat: reports, audits, budget changes, campaign builds. Changes wait for your approval. Agencies get one workspace per client.
**Limit:** it needs access to your accounts to be useful. It is not a design tool.

### Claude or ChatGPT: writing and thinking
**Best for:** first drafts, rewrites in a brand voice, research summaries, email sequences.
Both are excellent. Claude Pro is $20/mo. ChatGPT has a free plan and several paid tiers. Pick the one your team already likes; the difference between them matters less than the brief you give them.
**Limit:** on their own they can't see your accounts. Connect them to your data through MCP servers or an agent.

### Surfer: content that matches the SERP
**Best for:** writers who need to know what the top pages cover before they write.
**Limit:** a high content score doesn't guarantee a ranking. Pages need real expertise and first-hand proof, not only coverage of terms.

### Ahrefs or Semrush: the SEO data layer
**Best for:** keyword research, competitor research and backlinks. Ahrefs is stronger for links; Semrush is the broader suite.
**Limit:** API access, which you need to feed an agent or automated reports, depends on your plan. See our [full comparison](https://toffu.ai/blog/semrush-vs-ahrefs).

### Optmyzr: paid search rules and audits
**Best for:** PPC teams that want automated rules, audits and budget pacing across ad platforms.
**Limit:** it optimizes inside the ad platforms. Reporting and other channels still need another tool.

### Canva: visuals without a designer
**Best for:** social graphics, ad variations, quick resizes in your brand kit.
**Limit:** not for original concept work.

### ElevenLabs: voiceover
**Best for:** video ads, product demos and explainer voiceovers.
**Limit:** check the license terms before you clone a real person's voice.

### Google Analytics 4 and Search Console: the free data
Every tool above is only as good as the data under it. Both are free. If you are unsure your GA4 numbers are real, read our [GA4 traffic analysis guide](https://toffu.ai/blog/google-analytics-traffic-analysis).

## How to choose an AI marketing tool that improves results

Choose by the hours and the numbers a tool changes, not by its feature list. Then measure it.

1. **List the 5 tasks that take your team the most hours each week.** Pick tools for those, not for the categories in a roundup.
2. **Check API and integration access before you buy.** In 2026 the value of a tool is often in what it lets other tools do with its data.
3. **Test on real work.** Every tool here has a free plan or a trial. Run your actual weekly report or audit through it.
4. **Count the hand-offs.** If a task still needs you to move data between three tools, you need an agent, not another assistant.
5. **Measure one number before and after.** Pick the number the task affects, for example hours per client report, time to launch a campaign, or cost per lead. Write it down before the trial and check it again after four weeks. Keep the tool only if that number moved.

## FAQ

**What is the best AI tool for marketing?**
There is no single one. For writing, Claude or ChatGPT. For SEO data, Ahrefs or Semrush. For running campaigns and reports across accounts, an AI agent like Toffu.

**Are there free AI marketing tools?**
Yes: ChatGPT, Claude, Canva, ElevenLabs and Toffu have free plans, and Google Analytics and Search Console are free.

**What is the difference between an AI marketing tool and an AI marketing agent?**
A tool helps with one step. An agent takes a goal and does the steps across your connected accounts, then reports back.

**How much do AI marketing tools cost?**
The picks above range from free to about $130/mo for single tools. Agency platforms usually price per client.
