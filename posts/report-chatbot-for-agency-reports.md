# Your Clients Have Questions About Their Report. Now the Report Answers.

You send the monthly report on Monday. By Tuesday the questions start. "How much did we spend in October?" "Which campaign brought the most leads?" "Why is cost per lead up?"

Most of the answers are already in the report. The client just did not find them, or did not want to scroll. So someone on your team stops what they are doing, opens the report, finds the number, and writes it back. Multiply that by every client and every report, and a good part of the week goes to reading reports aloud.

Toffu's report chatbot takes that work. It sits at the bottom right of the report you shared, and it answers your client's questions from the report itself.

## What your client sees

Your client opens the report link as usual. A chat button waits at the bottom right corner, with your agency's logo on it. When they open it, a few suggested questions help them start. They can also type a question in their own words, and the answer comes from what the report shows.

When the answer is not in the report, the chatbot says so. It does not guess, and it does not make up a number. It sees only this report: it has no access to your clients' ad accounts, to your other reports, or to your conversations in Toffu.

It answers each person in the language they write in. The chat itself (its buttons, greeting and notes) can be in English or in Hebrew, and in Hebrew it reads right to left. On a phone it opens full screen.

## Your brand, not ours

The chat carries your agency's brand: its name, your logo, your color, your greeting and the tone you set. Toffu does not appear in it. Set the branding once and use it for every report of the client's workspace.

## You decide what it does with hard questions

Some questions are not for a chatbot. A client asks about budget for next quarter, or about a campaign you plan to change. In Behavior you list:

- **Topics to pass on to your team.** The chatbot does not answer them; it hands them to you.
- **Topics to avoid.**
- **Ask the team.** When this is on, a question the report cannot answer goes to your team, and the client can leave an email for the reply. Choose up to five addresses that get the question. It is off until you turn it on.

## See what your clients ask

The Log of each report shows what visitors asked and what the chatbot answered. The Client questions page shows the open questions from every report of the workspace in one list, and you mark each one answered when you reply.

What clients ask is useful by itself. If three clients ask the same thing, the next report should show it on the first page.

## Test it before your client does

The chatbot is off on every report until you turn it on. Open the report in Toffu and click **Chatbot** in the top bar. There are three modes:

1. **Off.** Nobody sees the chat.
2. **Preview.** Only your team sees the chat, inside Toffu, on the report. Ask it the questions your client would ask.
3. **On.** Anyone with the report link sees the chat. Your team still sees it in Toffu.

## For agency owners: you control where it is allowed

In AI Settings, the **Report chatbot** card sets the rule for all your client workspaces:

- **Allow for all clients**
- **Allow for selected clients**, with a list of the client workspaces to tick
- **Don't allow**

A client workspace sees one line: allowed or not allowed by your agency.
