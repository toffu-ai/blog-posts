*Updated 16 November 2026.*

You can find a competitor's backlinks in three ways:

1. **Free:** Bing Webmaster Tools shows backlinks to any domain, including sites you don't own, and compares them side by side.
2. **Paid:** Ahrefs (Site Explorer → Backlinks, then Link Intersect) or Semrush (Backlink Analytics, then Backlink Gap). These have the most complete data.
3. **By search:** find the pages that *mention* your competitors and not you. Most of these pages already link out, so they are the warmest outreach targets you will find.

The first two give you a list of links. The third gives you a list of people to email. Below are all three, and the results we got when we ran the third method on our own niche.

## Method 1: Bing Webmaster Tools (free)

Bing Webmaster Tools has a backlinks report that works on any domain, not only sites you verified.

1. Sign in to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters) and add your own site.
2. Open **Backlinks** and choose the option to compare with other sites.
3. Enter up to a few competitor domains.
4. Use the filter that shows domains linking to the competitor but **not** to you.
5. Export the list.

The limit: Bing shows a representative sample, not every link. That is enough to find patterns: which directories, which roundups, which communities link to your competitors. It is not enough for a full audit.

Google Search Console only shows links to sites you own, so it can't help here.

## Method 2: Ahrefs or Semrush (paid)

If you already pay for one of them, this is the fastest way:

**Ahrefs**
1. Site Explorer → enter the competitor → **Referring domains**. Sort by Domain Rating and set "Dofollow" only.
2. **Best by links** shows which competitor pages earn the most links. Those pages show you what your market links to: tools, data studies, templates.
3. **Link Intersect**: enter 2-3 competitors at the top, your domain at the bottom. You get the sites that link to them and not to you.

**Semrush**
1. Backlink Analytics → competitor domain → **Referring Domains**.
2. **Backlink Gap**: up to 5 domains side by side, filtered to "Best" prospects.

Two warnings from experience:
- **API access depends on your plan.** If you want an AI agent or a script to pull these lists for you, check your plan first. When we ran this pull from the Ahrefs key on our own workspace on 16 November 2026, the API answered `403 Insufficient plan`. Ahrefs API v3 needs Lite or higher ([Ahrefs help](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/6559232-about-api-v3)). Our [Ahrefs vs Semrush comparison](https://toffu.ai/blog/semrush-vs-ahrefs) covers the API rules for both tools.
- **Most of a competitor's links are worthless to you.** Scraper sites, old press releases and spam directories fill the long tail. Expect to keep only a small part of a raw list.

## Method 3: the mention gap (what we actually did)

Backlink tools show links that already exist. They miss the pages where a link to you is most likely: pages that write about your category, list your competitors, and leave you out.

We ran this for Toffu, an AI marketing agent for agencies, on 16 November 2026. We searched Google for the queries an agency owner would type ("best AI marketing agents for agencies", "best AI tools for PPC") and opened the roundups that ranked. For each page we wrote down three things: does it mention us, does it link out to the tools it lists, and when it was last updated.

| Roundup | Mentions Toffu | Links out to listed tools | Last updated |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Rework: Best AI agents for agencies](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-agents/best-ai-agents-for-agencies-2026) | No | Yes (12 of 13) | Aug 2026 |
| [Enrich Labs: Best AI for marketing agencies](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/best-ai-for-marketing-agencies-2026) | No | Yes (9 competitors) | Oct 2026 |
| [Dupple: Best AI PPC tools](https://dupple.com/learn/best-ai-ppc-tools) | No | Yes | Nov 2026 |
| [Supermetrics: AI agents for marketing](https://supermetrics.com/blog/ai-agents-for-marketing) | No | No (own pages only) | Sep 2026 |
| [FlowHunt: Best AI marketing agents](https://www.flowhunt.io/blog/best-ai-marketing-agents/) | No | No (own pages only) | May 2026 |

What the table tells us:

- **0 of 5 mention us.** That is the gap.
- **3 of 5 link out to every tool they list** and were updated in the last 4 months. Those 3 are the outreach list. A page that updates often and links out is a page that adds tools.
- **The 2 that don't link out are written by vendors** with their own agent. We skip them. A vendor won't add a competitor to its own list.
- **Jasper appears in 4 of the 5.** If you sell in a crowded category, look at which brand shows up everywhere and find out why.

This took about 20 minutes by hand. It needs no paid tool.

## How to qualify a link before you chase it

Keep a prospect only if all four are true:

1. **Real traffic.** The page ranks for something. Paste the URL into any free traffic checker, or look at whether it ranks for its own title.
2. **Topical match.** The page writes about your category. A marketing-tools roundup linking to an agency tool is natural. A pet blog is not.
3. **Updated in the last 12 months.** Old pages rarely get edited.
4. **A named person.** You can find who writes or maintains it.

## What to send

Short and specific. A template:

```
Hi [name], I'm [you] from [product], a [category] tool for [audience].
Your [page name] lists [competitor A] and [competitor B].
[Product] does [one specific thing they don't].
If it's useful for your readers, I can set you up with an account to test it
before your next update.
```

No link-swap offers. No "I loved your article". One specific reason the page is better for its readers with you on it.

## Make it a monthly habit

Links come and go. Once a month:

1. Re-run the mention-gap search for your top 3 category queries. New roundups appear every month.
2. In Ahrefs or Semrush, check the competitor's **new referring domains** for the last 30 days. Fresh links show where your competitors are doing outreach now.
3. Log every prospect, the date you contacted them and the result. After 3 months you will know which kind of page says yes.

## FAQ

**How do I check competitor backlinks for free?**
Use Bing Webmaster Tools. Its backlinks report works on domains you don't own and can compare several sites. The data is a sample, not the full list.

**What is a backlink gap?**
The list of sites that link to one or more of your competitors but not to you. Ahrefs calls the report Link Intersect. Semrush calls it Backlink Gap.

**How many competitor backlinks are worth pursuing?**
A small part of a raw list. Filter by traffic, topic, freshness and whether a person maintains the page.

**Is copying competitor backlinks against Google's rules?**
No. Asking a relevant page to mention you is normal outreach. Paying for links or trading links at scale is what Google's spam policies target.
