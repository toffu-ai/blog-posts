# Toffu now has a browser, and an email address

Most of what your agency asks Toffu for goes through integrations: Google Ads, Meta, GA4 and the rest. But some of the work never had an API. The client's monthly Looker Studio report. A setting in an ad platform that only the UI exposes. A client portal someone set up years ago.

Until now, that work came back to you. Export the report, paste the numbers, click through the screens yourself.

Two new things change that.

## 1. Toffu has its own browser

When a task needs a website, Toffu opens a browser and does it, step by step, like a person would. Ask "read October's numbers from the client's Looker report" and it opens the report, finds the page, and reads them.

**You can watch it work.** The browser opens in a panel next to the chat, live. You see each page as Toffu sees it. The panel also has a Browser button, so you can open it at any time while Toffu works.

**You step in only when it needs you.** Some steps are only yours: a login, a two-factor code, a captcha. Toffu stops and asks. You do that step in the same panel, on desktop or on your phone, then click Done. Toffu continues from where it stopped. It never asks you for a password, and it never types one.

**Logins are kept for later runs.** When you sign in to a site in Toffu's browser, the login is saved for you. Next week's scheduled report starts already signed in, with no one at the keyboard. You see every saved login under AI Settings → Browser logins, and you can remove any of them. Each one expires after 30 days without use.

**It handles the small things.** Cookie banners are refused for you, so they don't block the page.

Meta Ads works this way: sign in once in Toffu's browser, and later runs use that login.

## 2. Toffu has its own email address

Many of the tools behind this work sit behind a Google login: Looker Studio, Google Ads, Search Console. Until now, someone had to sign in for Toffu.

Now each workspace gets its own address, like **acme@agent.toffu.ai**. You find it in the browser panel, with a copy button. If you run a workspace per client, each client's workspace has its own address. You invite the address of the workspace that does the work.

**Invite it like a teammate.** Share the client's Looker report with it. Add it as a user in the Google Ads account. Use the normal Share or Invite button you'd use for a colleague, at the access level you choose.

**Toffu signs in by itself.** When Toffu's browser reaches a Google sign-in page, it signs in as its own address. No password from you, no step in the chat. That includes scheduled runs at 7am on the first of the month.

**It sees only what you share.** It is a separate account, not yours. It has no access to anything you haven't shared with it. To take access away, unshare the report or remove the user, the same way you would for anyone else.

If Toffu opens something it can't see yet, it tells you which address to share it with. You share it once, and from then on it opens.

The address is for invitations and sharing. It doesn't receive email.

## Why it's secure

**One address per workspace, never shared.** Each workspace's address belongs to that workspace only. A report you share with one client's address is not visible from another client's workspace. Clients stay as separate as they are in your agency today.

**There is no password to leak.** The account has no password that anyone knows, us included. It signs in only through Toffu's own sign-in, and only from Toffu's browser. Each sign-in uses a one-time code that works once and expires within minutes.

**It can never be you.** Toffu's sign-in works only for its own agent addresses. It cannot sign in as you, your team or anyone at your clients.

**It sees only what you grant.** It starts with access to nothing. It gets exactly the reports and accounts you share with it, at the level you choose, Viewer included. You can see who has access, and remove it, in the tool itself, as for any teammate.

**Your own logins stay yours.** A login you do yourself in Toffu's browser is saved for you alone. Teammates in the same workspace can't see or use it, and you can remove it in AI Settings → Browser logins.

## What the two do together

Take the monthly client report built in Looker Studio, with no API behind it.

1. You share the report with your workspace's Toffu address. Once.
2. You ask Toffu to read the month's numbers and write the summary. It opens the report in its browser, signs in by itself, reads the pages and writes it up.
3. You schedule it. Next month it runs on its own: same report, same sign-in, no step from you.

The same applies to a Google Ads task that only the UI supports, or a client's dashboard in any other web tool where you saved a login once.

## How to start

Ask Toffu to do something in a tool it has no integration for, and it will open the browser. For Google tools, copy the address from the browser panel and share the report or account with it first.
